About

Since 2024, I have been working as a freelance developer. At Wujo, I designed over 20 screens and helped turn the traditional Ethiopian ROSCA-based system into an award-winning product. I worked across React.js, Next.js, Node.js, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS. I also built a system for patient registration, record lookup, and file management, which replaced more than 500 paper records and made manual lookups much faster.

Currently, I work as a web developer for Emmanuel Disciples Church, where I built the church's first website, giving the congregation an online presence for the first time. Whether working on a team or building products , I bring that same community mindset, and I hope that in time, people see me as someone who genuinely cares about what technology can do for the people around them.
