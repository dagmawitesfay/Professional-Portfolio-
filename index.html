Introduction

About

As a freelance developer, I helped turn Iqub, a traditional Ethiopian community savings practice, into a mobile app, which placed second in the ALX Startup Accelerator program. When I saw that Emmanuel Disciples Church had no website, I took the initiative to build its first one, giving members another way to find information beyond Sunday announcements.

Currently, I’m working with the Ethiopian Evangelical Church in Boston on a new website that will let their 44,000 YouTube subscribers join Bible study groups, register for retreats, and stay up to date on events.
